# CAN-08NM050

A dataframe of annual maximum series observations for station 08NM050,
OKANAGAN RIVER AT PENTICTON in British Columbia, Canada.

## Usage

``` r
CAN_08NM050
```

## Format

A dataframe with 97 rows and 2 columns spanning the period 1921-2017.

## Source

Meteorological Service of Canada (MSC) GeoMet Platform

## Details

Variables:

- `max`: Numeric; the observed annual maximum series, in m\\^3\\/s.

- `year`: Integer; the corresponding years.

## Additional Information

This is a regulated station that is not part of the RHBN. The Okanagan
River upstream of the station has been regulated since 1914 due to the
construction of the first dam, followed by a second dam in 1920, and a
regulation system in the early 1950s, consisting of four dams and 38 km
of engineered channel. Rapid human settlement, development, and
agricultural activity have also occurred in the watershed.

This dataset exhibits serial correlation and trends in both the mean and
variability. Since the station is heavily influenced by reservoir
operations, this dataset is intended for teaching purposes—not for
practical flood estimation.
